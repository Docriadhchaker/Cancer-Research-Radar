# Scientific Safety & Non-Prescriptive Policy

## Non-Clinical Scope

Cancer Research Radar is an exploratory biomedical informatics tool and technical Proof of Concept designed to facilitate landscape discovery of oncology research strategies, active clinical trials, and regulatory approvals.

## Core Rules

1. **No Treatment Recommendations**:
   - The platform never recommends, prescribes, or ranks therapeutic interventions for individual patient decision-making.

2. **No Efficacy Inferences**:
   - High clinical trial volume, publication frequency, or emerging momentum scores must NOT be interpreted as evidence of clinical efficacy or superiority over established standards of care.

3. **No Eligibility Determinations**:
   - Clinical trial eligibility summaries are provided strictly for research orientation. Actual trial eligibility requires thorough institutional clinical review and consent procedures.

4. **Zero Data Fabrication**:
   - When registry fields (such as Phase, Sponsor, or Location) are absent from source records, the system displays `"Not reported"` rather than inferring values.

5. **Transparent Evidence Provenance**:
   - Every metric, milestone, paper, and trial links directly to its source registry (ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, or FDA).
